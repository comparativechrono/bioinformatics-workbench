Notices for the files in this folder
====================================

These are the tutorial files of HMMER 3.4 (the folder tutorial/ of hmmer-3.4.tar.gz, http://hmmer.org/), unchanged and
under their own names, so that the tutorial of the HMMER User's Guide can be followed here as it is written. HMMER is
copyright (C) Sean R. Eddy, President and Fellows of Harvard College, Howard Hughes Medical Institute and others, and
distributed under the BSD 3-clause licence (LICENSE, HMMER's own).

  globins4.sto, globins4.hmm      four globins aligned (from the structural alignment of Bashford and colleagues, as the
                                  User's Guide says), and the profile HMM built from them
  globins45.fa, HBB_HUMAN         45 globins, and human beta-haemoglobin, to search
  fn3.sto, fn3.hmm                Pfam's fibronectin type III domain: seed alignment PF00041.20, profile PF00041.13
  Pkinase.sto, Pkinase.hmm        Pfam's protein kinase domain: seed alignment PF00069.24, profile PF00069.17
  7LESS_DROME                     the UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot record of the Drosophila Sevenless receptor (P13368; UniProt,
                                  CC BY 4.0): fibronectin type III domains and a kinase domain. HMMER reads the record
                                  as it is.
  MADE1.sto, MADE1.hmm            the human DNA transposon MADE1, Dfam DF0000629.2
  dna_target.fa                   330 kb of human chromosome 1, with copies of MADE1 (for nhmmer)

Pfam and Dfam data are free to use (CC0); cite Pfam (Mistry et al., Nucleic Acids Res 2021) and Dfam (Storer et al.,
Mobile DNA 2021) when you use them in your work. Where every file came from, and its SHA-256: provenance.json.
