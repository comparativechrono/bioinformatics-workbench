Notices for the files in this folder
====================================

Genotypes for PLINK 2: one file of real public data, and synthetic files for association tests.

  1kg-phase3-chr21.vcf.gz     real data: the first 600 biallelic variants of chromosome 21 (positions 9,411,239 to
                              9,425,829, GRCh37) of the 1000 Genomes Project's Phase 3 release, for all its 2,504
                              people, phased – the test file of PLINK 2 itself (plink-ng, 2.0/Tests/TEST_PHASED_VCF/
                              1kg_phase3_chr21_start.vcf.gz), unchanged. The sample names are the project's public IDs.
  keep48.samples.txt          the first 48 of those sample IDs (#IID and one per line), in the file's order – a small
                              set for --keep, so that tables of pairs stay small; not a sample of anything
  synthetic_scores.tsv        20 variants of the 1000 Genomes file (those with the most ALT alleles), each with an
                              allele and an arbitrary weight from -0.375 to 0.375: made up to try --score. Not effect
                              sizes, not a risk score of anything.
  synthetic_glm.vcf           SYNTHETIC: 603 invented samples (s0 … s602) and 30 variants (v1 … v30) on an invented
  synthetic_phenotypes.tsv    chromosome 1, with missing genotypes in a fixed pattern; three phenotypes – BINARY
  synthetic_covariates.tsv    (case 2 / control 1, drawn at random), QUANT (a random number) and FIRTH (case exactly when
                              the sample carries the ALT allele of v1: made so that logistic regression meets complete
                              separation and Firth regression is needed) – and two random covariates, C1 and C2.
                              Generated by formulas from PLINK 2's own tests (TEST_GLM_LOGISTIC_MISSING): no genotype
                              is associated with BINARY or QUANT except by chance.

The files were prepared by the genomics-wasm project (https://github.com/comparativechrono/genomics-wasm, commit
8eea96b: datasets/plink2), which records how each was made; here they are unchanged, and three of them renamed
(provenance.json).

The 1000 Genomes data: the International Genome Sample Resource makes the Phase 3 data available without embargo and asks
that it be cited (https://www.internationalgenome.org/IGSR_disclaimer/): The 1000 Genomes Project Consortium (2015) A
global reference for human genetic variation. Nature 526: 68–74. https://doi.org/10.1038/nature15393
The synthetic files come from PLINK 2's test recipes, which are under the GNU GPL 3 or later
(../../../assets/vendor/licenses/GPL-3.0.txt); the formulas, the seeds and the changes from upstream are recorded in
assets/vendor/licenses/genomics-wasm-build/datasets/plink2/.

They are for learning how the program works. The synthetic phenotypes, covariates and weights mean nothing biological or
clinical, and 600 variants of one chromosome are far too few to say anything about the people of the 1000 Genomes
Project.
