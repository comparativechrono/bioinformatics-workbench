Notices for the files in this folder
====================================

These are test files of MACS (Model-based Analysis of ChIP-Seq; https://github.com/macs3-project/MACS, the folder test/
at tag v3.0.5, commit c5443190e3edfeb301cc94acf450e2b2c026a223), unchanged and under their own names, so that the
commands of MACS's own tests can be run here as they are written. MACS is copyright (c) 2025 the macs3 project team and
distributed under the BSD 3-clause licence (LICENSE.MACS3, MACS's own); its test data has no licence of its own.

  CTCF_SE_ChIP_chr22_50k.bed.gz    49,622 reads of a ChIP-seq of CTCF, on human chromosome 22 (positions of hg19 /
                                   GRCh37): BED, one read of 101 bases per line, with its strand
  CTCF_SE_CTRL_chr22_50k.bed.gz    50,837 reads of its control (the input: the same chromatin, without the antibody)
  CTCF_PE_ChIP_chr22_50k.bedpe.gz  the same two experiments read from both ends: a fragment per line – chromosome, start
  CTCF_PE_CTRL_chr22_50k.bedpe.gz  and end (MACS's BEDPE, not the BEDPE of bedtools)
  run_callpeak_narrow0_peaks.narrowPeak
                                   the peaks that MACS's test expects from
                                     macs3 callpeak -g 52000000 -t CTCF_SE_ChIP_chr22_50k.bed.gz -c CTCF_SE_CTRL_chr22_50k.bed.gz
                                         -n run_callpeak_narrow0 -B --cutoff-analysis
                                   (test/standard_results_callpeak_narrow/ of the repository)

The files hold the positions of the reads only – no sequences. The repository names their origin only by their file
names; its change log describes a larger CTCF test set (5 million reads, not included here) as a subsample of an ENCODE
CTCF ChIP-seq. To cite MACS: Doherty P, Hu Q, Zhuang Z, Zhang H, Penikalapati SC, Liu S, Liu T (2026) MACS3: A
Peak-calling Platform for Bulk and Single-cell Regulatory Genomics. Genomics, Proteomics & Bioinformatics, qzag097
(doi:10.1093/gpbjnl/qzag097); and Zhang Y et al. (2008) Model-based Analysis of ChIP-Seq (MACS). Genome Biology 9:
R137. Where every file came from, its SHA-256 and its git blob: provenance.json.
