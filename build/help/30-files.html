Your files

Your files are in your home folder, ~ (/home/user). The notebooks, the terminal, Galaxy and the Files tab all use the same files.

Where your files are kept

There is no server: your files are kept in this browser, on this computer (in its storage for web sites, IndexedDB). They are there when you come back – in the same browser, on the same computer, at the same web address.

Download what you want to keep
  • Another browser, another computer or a private window does not have your files.
  • Clearing the browser's data for this site (cookies and site data) deletes them – some computers do that by themselves when you log out (shared university PCs often do).
  • Browsers may delete site data when the disk is nearly full.

My files → Download all my files (.zip) (top right) keeps everything; download single notebooks with Download in the Notebook tab.

The dot at the top right, Saved in this browser, shows that the latest changes are stored. My files also says how much space you use; browsers give a site a share of the free disk space – usually several gigabytes.

The Files tab

  • Open a file by clicking it. Text files open in an editor (with colours for Python, R, shell, YAML …): edit, then Save or Ctrl+S. Several files open as tabs.
  • Other files are shown in a way that fits them: tables (CSV, TSV) as tables, FASTA files with their sequences, alignments in colour, trees (.nwk) drawn, pictures, PDF files (with zoom and page numbers; their text can be selected and copied), HTML reports (without their scripts). Open as text shows the text itself.
  • Notebooks (.ipynb) open in the Notebook tab, structures (.pdb, .cif) in the 3D viewer, networks (.sif, .graphml, .xgmml, .gml, .cyjs, .cx2) in the Networks tab.
  • The ⋯ of a file or folder: open in another tab (Notebook, 3D viewer, Genome browser, Networks, Galaxy), download, rename, duplicate, copy the path, open the folder in the terminal, unzip, delete. A table (CSV, TSV) opens in Networks as a table of edges, or as values for the nodes of the network that is open.
  • The buttons at the top: upload files from your computer, a new file, a new folder, show hidden files (names that start with a dot).

Upload and download

  • Upload: drag files from your computer onto the Files tab, or use the upload button or My files → Add files from my computer…. Folders can be uploaded too. A .zip can be unpacked with its ⋯ menu (or unzip in the terminal).
  • Download: the ⋯ of a file; a folder downloads as a .zip. In the terminal: download FILE.
  • Very large files (several gigabytes) do not fit: work on a part of them (head, samtools view -s, seqtk sample) on your own computer first.

The example data

/data/examples comes with the workbench and cannot be changed. The Files tab shows it under Examples (read-only); its README.md says where each file comes from. To change a file, copy it first – its ⋯ menu has Copy to my files, or in the terminal:

cp -r /data/examples/reads ~/example-reads
ls -l ~/example-reads

Start afresh

My files → Start afresh deletes all your files, Galaxy's history and the workbench's settings in this browser – after you have downloaded what you want to keep. The settings of the AI assistant and of the help pane are kept.
