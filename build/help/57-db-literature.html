Papers and small molecules

Europe PMC searches the life-science literature; PubChem describes small molecules – drugs, metabolites, ligands – and gives their 3D models.

Europe PMC

Europe PMC holds the abstracts of PubMed and more – preprints, patents, guidelines – and millions of full texts. Type search terms; tick Only open access to keep the papers whose full text is open – free to read, with a licence that allows reuse (many more papers are free to read without such a licence). The table lists the most relevant first, with the journal, year, how often each has been cited and whether it is open access; click a title to open the paper.

Its query syntax narrows a search:

SearchFinds
CYP2C19 clopidogrelpapers that mention both
"variant effect predictor"the exact phrase
AlphaFold AND PUB_TYPE:reviewreviews only
TITLE:"long-read sequencing" AND PUB_YEAR:[2024 TO 2026]the phrase in the title, in recent years
AUTH:"Jumper J" AND AlphaFoldpapers by an author (initials after the surname) on a topic – an author's name alone also finds others with that name

A search finds papers; reading them is still your job. Check what kind of paper it is (a trial, a review, a case report), how large the study was, and whether others have since confirmed it.

PubChem

PubChem (NCBI) is the largest open collection of chemical information. Type the name of a compound or its PubChem CID (compound ID): the answer has its 2D drawing and its properties – formula, molecular weight, IUPAC name, SMILES, XLogP (how fat-soluble it is), and how many hydrogen-bond donors and acceptors it has.

  • Show in the 3D viewer loads PubChem's 3D model (a computed shape, as an .sdf file).
  • Save the 3D model (.sdf) keeps it in ~/structures.

The ligands in a PDB structure are named by three-letter codes (HEM); its Protein Data Bank entry lists them, with their names.
