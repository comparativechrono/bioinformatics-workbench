Viewer commands

The command line under the 3D picture takes these commands. Several can go on one line, separated by ; – a script that rebuilds a picture exactly. ↑ brings back earlier commands.

What a command acts on

Many commands end with what they act on; without it they act on the selection, or on everything when nothing is selected.

WriteFor
A · chain Achain A
A:64 · A:60-70 · A:His64a residue, a range of residues
HEMa ligand, by its name in the workspace
ligands · water · ions · sugars · polymer · allkinds of molecule
A or HEMboth
selethe selection
3FXI Achain A of one of several loaded structures

Scripts

Each button runs a script in the viewer – copy the commands into the command line to change them:

load 1MBN; hide all; show cartoon; color ss; only sticks HEM; color element HEM; site HEM 4.5; center HEM
load 1MBN; color rainbow; show sticks A:64 or A:93; label A:64 or A:93; distance A:64.NE2 A:93.NE2

The AlphaFold model of sperm whale myoglobin, laid on the experimental structure:

load 1MBN; load af P02185; superpose AF-P02185-F1:A onto 1MBN:A
