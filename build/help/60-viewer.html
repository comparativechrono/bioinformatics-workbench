The 3D viewer

The 3D viewer shows the structures of proteins, nucleic acids and small molecules – from the Protein Data Bank, from AlphaFold DB or from your files. Style and colour them, find binding sites, measure distances, and save pictures for your reports.

Load a structure

  • PDB ID (top left): type 1MBN and Load – fetched from the RCSB PDB.
  • AlphaFold: a UniProt accession and Load model – the predicted structure, coloured by its confidence (see AlphaFold DB).
  • From your files: click a .pdb, .cif or .sdf file in the Files tab, or type open FILE.pdb in the terminal. Open file takes one from your computer.
  • From the Databases tab: Load in 3D in the Protein Data Bank card, Show in the 3D viewer in AlphaFold DB and PubChem, 3D buttons in a UniProt entry.

The workspace on the left lists what is loaded – each structure with its chains and ligands. Click a name to select it, double-click to centre on it, and use the eye to show or hide it. Several structures can be loaded together.

Styles and colours

The tabs above the picture – Display, Colour, Select, Labels & key, Measure, Views, Save image – hold the tools. Styles and colours apply to the selection, or to everything when nothing is selected: the bar above the picture always says which.

StyleShows
Cartoonthe fold of the backbone: helices as spirals, strands as arrows, loops as tubes
Tubea smooth line through the backbone
Sticks, Ball & stickevery bond – for ligands and side chains
Spheresatoms at their real size: the space a molecule fills
Surface, See-throughthe molecular surface – shape, pockets and grooves; see-through shows what is inside
Linesthin bonds

Colour schemes: by chain, by molecule, rainbow (blue at the N-terminus to red at the C-terminus), secondary structure, hydrophobicity (orange hydrophobic, blue hydrophilic), residue type (acidic red, basic blue, polar green, hydrophobic sandy), element, pLDDT (AlphaFold models), B-factor (experimental structures), or one colour of your choice.

Select

Click atoms, names in the workspace, or letters in the sequence bar under the picture (it shows the sequence of one chain; choose which). The Select tab also has Box select (drag a rectangle), Select nearby (residues within some ångström of the selection), and a box for a selection query such as A:64 (residue 64 of chain A), A:60-70, HEM.

Binding sites and contacts

Select a ligand – click HEM in the workspace – and use Binding site: the residues around it are shown as sticks, with lines for the contacts they make: hydrogen bonds, salt bridges, π-stacking, cation–π, halogen bonds and metal coordination. Hydrophobic contacts are many, and are left out: the command contacts HEM hydrophobic adds them.

Measure, label, compare

  • Measure: click two atoms for the distance between them, in ångström.
  • Labels & key: labels on residues, a title on the picture, and a colour key (legend).
  • Views: Save view remembers the picture – orientation, styles, colours and labels – like a slide; click it to go back to it.
  • Superpose: lay one structure on another (an AlphaFold model on an experimental structure); the RMSD says how far apart they are. In the model's panel: Superpose onto…, or the command superpose.

The command line

Under the picture, Command line opens a command line: load 1MBN, color rainbow A, show sticks HEM; type help for the list. Most buttons write the command they stand for there too – a record of how you made a picture. All the commands are in the command reference.

Save your work

  • Save image: a PNG picture at 1×–4× the screen size, on the current or a transparent background, with or without the labels and key. Download PNG saves it to your computer; PNG to my files keeps it in ~/figures, for a notebook or a report; Copy image puts it on the clipboard for Word or PowerPoint.
  • Coordinates: a structure as it is shown – superposed, if it was – as a .pdb file, downloaded or kept in ~/structures.
  • Views → Save session downloads everything – structures, styles, labels, views – as one file; Open session continues from it.

The structures, styles and views are not kept when the page is reloaded: save a session to continue later.
