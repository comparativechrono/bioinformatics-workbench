Your data and privacy

The workbench has no server of its own that keeps or sees your work: it runs in your browser. What leaves your computer goes to the public services you use, and only when you use them.

What is kept on your computer

  • Your files – notebooks, results, uploads – and Galaxy's datasets: in this browser's storage for the site (IndexedDB).
  • In the browser's local storage: your last 200 terminal commands; the list of Galaxy's history and its workflows; settings – the open tab and notebook, the help page, the genome, the choices of the AI assistant (not its key), and your e-mail address for BLAST if you ask to remember it.
  • The AI key: only in the tab's memory, or, if you tick it, until the tab is closed (session storage). Never in a file or in long-term storage.

On a shared computer, the next person who uses the same browser account can see what is stored: download your work, then My files → Start afresh. That deletes your files, Galaxy's history, the terminal's commands and the remembered e-mail address; the choices of the AI assistant and of the help pane stay, and the AI key is forgotten when you close the tab. The workbench itself sets no cookies and has no tracking or analytics.

Personal and patient data

The workbench is for learning, with public data. Never put personal or patient data into it – not into the Databases tab, not into an AI tool, not into your files here. Data you have been given for your studies stays within the rules you were given with it.
