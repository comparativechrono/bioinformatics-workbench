Programs and versions

Results depend on versions: cite the ones you used, with the papers of the programs (To cite; in full at the end of each program's help page). help in the terminal prints this list too; NAME --version one program's.

The terminal's bioinformatics programs

The real programs, compiled to WebAssembly (most by the Biowasm project; BWA, BWA-MEM2, HISAT2, FreeBayes, featureCounts, StringTie, kallisto, Salmon, mosdepth, Cutadapt, LoFreq, SomaticSniper, Strelka2, Kraken2, Bracken, bustools, Prodigal, HMMER and PLINK 2 by the genomics-wasm project). Galaxy runs the same ones.
